The problem

Generic advice is easy to find. Relevant experience is not.

When a real decision lands on your desk (pricing, market entry, a first enterprise deal) the usual routes fall short. LinkedIn connections may know you, not your problem. Well-meaning advisors offer frameworks that were never tested against your situation.

What actually moves a founder forward is talking to someone who has already been through that specific challenge, in that specific market, and can tell you plainly what happened.

The usual routes

  • Ask in a founder group chat and hope
  • Read one more generic playbook

What the moment calls for

  • Advice for the decision in front of you, not in general
